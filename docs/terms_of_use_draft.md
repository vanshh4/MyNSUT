## 3. Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

MyNSUT is designed to foster academic productivity, cohort coordination, and campus engagement. All users must adhere to the highest standards of academic integrity and digital citizenship.

### 3.1 Permitted Uses
You may use MyNSUT to:
- Access and manage your academic class workspace, announcements, and task trackers.
- Discover, bookmark, and register for approved campus society events, hackathons, and cultural fests.
- Share and download academic study materials, previous year examination question papers (PYQs), lecture summaries, and reference resources.
- View official university circulars, datesheets, and notices.
- Build your verified student profile and discover campus peers across branches.

### 3.2 Strictly Prohibited Conduct
You agree that you will **NOT**, under any circumstances:

#### A. Impersonation & Deception
- Impersonate any fellow student, faculty member, Class Representative (CR), society core member, or university administrator.
- Register an account using another student's UMS roll number or misrepresent your academic branch, section, or admission year.

#### B. Academic Dishonesty & Copyright Infringement
- Post, upload, or transmit live examination question papers, confidential answer keys, graded assignment solutions, or materials that violate NSUT's Academic Integrity Code.
- Upload copyrighted textbooks, software, or course packs without explicit authorization from the copyright holder.

#### C. Harassment, Abuse & Misuse of Feeds
- Post harassing, defamatory, vulgar, threatening, sexually explicit, hateful, or discriminatory content in class announcements, task boards, or society discussions.
- Use society broadcast features or class announcement tools for spam, commercial advertising, partisan political campaigning, or unauthorized fundraising.

#### D. Technical Abuse & Platform Tampering
- Attempt to exploit, probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Platform, reverse-proxy rewrite rules, or backend API endpoints.
- Attempt to circumvent or defeat Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), authorization middlewares, rate limits, or CSRF guards.
- Deploy automated crawlers, scrapers, bots, or automated submission scripts against the Platform without explicit written permission.
- Introduce malicious software, viruses, worms, Trojan horses, or logic bombs designed to disrupt university infrastructure.
